# Contributing to NoVis

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **NoVis**. We welcome community contributions of all kinds — whether you're fixing bugs, improving performance, adding features, or helping with documentation and testing.

Our goal is to build a secure, transparent, and privacy-respecting communication platform. Every contribution matters.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Fork** the repository.
2. Create a new branch for your changes.
3. Make your edits and ensure the codebase remains functional and clean.
4. Open a **Pull Request (PR)** with a clear explanation of:
   - What your change does
   - Why it’s necessary
   - Any known implications or risks

---

## Contribution Guidelines

- Keep all changes **modular** and **well-documented**.
- Prioritize **security, readability, and maintainability**.
- Write clear commit messages and PR titles.
- Be respectful and constructive in discussions — this is a collaborative space.

---

## Code Style

- Java: just make sure we can understand it. may make your PRs more likely to be accepted.
- JavaScript/TypeScript: Follows ESLint + Prettier (config provided)
- Python: Use `black` and `flake8`
- Aim for clean, modular architecture and minimal duplication
- Avoid including large third-party dependencies unless necessary

---
